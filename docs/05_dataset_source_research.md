# DATASET SOURCE RESEARCH

## Project 01: Government Budget Transparency & Spending Efficiency Analytics

**Research Date:** 31 October 2026  
**Research Status:** Initial Source Research Completed  
**Primary Country:** Bangladesh

## 1. Research Objective

The objective of this research is to identify reliable government budget and expenditure data sources for analyzing budget allocation, actual expenditure, ministry-wise spending, and budget utilization.

## 2. Primary Data Sources

### Source 01: Ministry of Finance, Bangladesh

Website: https://mof.gov.bd/pages/budget-mofs

Available document categories include:

- Budget at a Glance
- Annual Financial Statements
- Demands for Grants and Appropriations
- Detailed Development Budget
- Budget Execution Reports
- Annual Budget Implementation Reports

Purpose:
- Budget allocation research
- Ministry-wise budget research
- Government expenditure analysis

### Source 02: Finance Division Annual Budget Reports

Website: https://mof.gov.bd/pages/reports

Identified reports:

- FY 2024-25: Statement 1 & 2 (Yearly)
- FY 2022-23: Statement 1 & 2 (Yearly)
- FY 2021-22: Statement 1 & 2 (Yearly)
- FY 2020-21: Statement 1 & 2 (Yearly)
- FY 2018-19: Statement 1 & 2 (Yearly)

### Source 03: iBAS++

Website: https://ibas.finance.gov.bd/portal/accountsReport

Purpose:
- Government financial reporting reference
- Broad expenditure classification research

Research Note:
The Accounts at a Glance page currently indicates that it is under construction. It is not selected as the primary downloadable dataset at this stage.

### Source 04: World Bank

Website: https://data.worldbank.org/indicator/GF.XPD.BUDG.ZS?locations=BD

Indicator:
Primary government expenditures as a proportion of original approved budget (%).

Purpose:
- Supporting country-level budget execution benchmark
- External comparison and reference

Limitation:
This indicator is not a ministry-level expenditure dataset.

## 3. Selected Primary Dataset

### Dataset A

Name: Statement 1 — Budget and Accounts at a Glance

Financial Year: 2024-25

Source: Ministry of Finance, Bangladesh

Unit: Taka in Crore

Identified fields:

- Revised Budget
- Actual Expenditure
- Budget Balance
- Actual / Budget Percentage
- Operating Expenditure
- Development Expenditure

Official PDF:
https://mof.gov.bd/pages/reports/

### Dataset B

Name: Statement 2 — Comparison of Consolidated Fund Income and Expenditures: Ministry/Division Wise

Financial Year: 2024-25

Source: Ministry of Finance, Bangladesh

Unit: Taka in Thousands

Identified fields:

- Ministry/Division Code
- Ministry/Division Description
- Targeted Revenue
- Actual Revenue
- Total Budget
- Total Actual Expenditure
- Operating Expenditure
- Development Expenditure

Official PDF:
https://mof.gov.bd/pages/reports/

## 4. Dataset Suitability

The FY 2024-25 Statement 2 is selected as the primary candidate dataset because it contains ministry-wise budget and expenditure information.

Statement 1 will be used as a supporting source for overall government budget and expenditure summaries.

## 5. Data Limitations

1. The identified reports are marked Draft / Draft Version.
2. Multiple financial years must be collected for historical trend analysis.
3. Statement 1 and Statement 2 use different monetary units.
4. Sector-level and program-level fields have not yet been confirmed.
5. Financial definitions and reporting coverage must be validated.
6. Missing or inconsistent values must be investigated before analysis.

## 6. Research Decision

Selected Primary Candidate:
FY 2024-25 Statement 2 — Ministry/Division Wise Expenditure.

Supporting Candidate:
FY 2024-25 Statement 1 — Budget and Accounts at a Glance.

Further research is required to collect comparable historical reports and confirm additional classification fields.

**End of Dataset Source Research**
