Project 01 — Government Budget Analytics Project
Phase 01: Business Understanding
Step 02 — Stakeholder Analysis
1. Primary Stakeholders

Primary Stakeholders are the individuals or organizations who will directly use the dashboard for budget monitoring, expenditure analysis, and financial decision-making.

Stakeholder	Role	Information Required
Ministry of Finance	Overall Budget Monitoring	Total Budget, Actual Expenditure, Budget Utilization
Government Ministries	Ministry-wise Budget Monitoring	Allocated Budget, Actual Spending, Budget Variance
Government Departments	Department-wise Performance Analysis	Department Expenditure, Spending Performance
Auditors	Financial Audit and Compliance	Budget vs. Expenditure, Underutilization, Overspending, Data Inconsistency
2. Secondary Stakeholders

Secondary Stakeholders may not directly use the dashboard as primary users, but they benefit from the project's analysis, insights, and findings.

Stakeholder	Role	Information Required
Senior Policymakers	Strategic Planning and Decision-Making	Sector-wise Budget Allocation, Spending Trends
Parliamentary Oversight Committees	Government Spending Oversight	Government Expenditure and Financial Performance
Citizens / Taxpayers	Public Budget Transparency	Sector-wise Budget Allocation and Actual Expenditure

3. Stakeholder Requirements

Each stakeholder has different business needs. The dashboard should provide relevant information and analysis according to their requirements.

A. Ministry of Finance
What is the total government budget?
What is the total actual expenditure?
What is the overall budget utilization rate?
Which ministries have overutilized or underutilized their allocated budgets?
How has expenditure changed compared to the previous financial year?
B. Government Ministries
What is the allocated budget for our ministry?
How much has actually been spent?
Which projects or programs have the highest expenditure?
What is the budget variance?
C. Government Departments
What is the department-wise expenditure?
Which departments have higher or lower budget utilization?
What is the spending performance of each department?
D. Auditors
Where are the major differences between allocated budgets and actual expenditure?
Which departments have recurring underutilization or overspending?
Are there any missing or inconsistent financial records?
E. Senior Policymakers
Which sectors require additional budget allocation?
Which departments need a review of their spending patterns?
How can budget planning be improved for the next financial year?
F. Parliamentary Oversight Committees
How has the government utilized its allocated budget?
Which sectors or departments have the highest expenditure?
What is the overall government spending performance?
G. Citizens / Taxpayers
How much budget is allocated to sectors such as Education, Healthcare, and Agriculture?
How much has actually been spent?
How has government spending changed over time?
